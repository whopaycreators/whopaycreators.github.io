Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: October 19, 2026

You can ask Letter Helper to delete your data at any time. Here is how:

By email

Email yim77708@gmail.com with the subject "Letter Helper data deletion" and include the WhatsApp phone number you used, so we can find your records.

What gets deleted

We delete the data we hold about you: your hashed phone identifier, your usage records, and any of your letters or messages we have stored.

How long it takes

Your data will be deleted within 30 days of your request.

Good to know

  • Messages stay in your own WhatsApp app until you delete them there.
  • Payment records held by Stripe are kept under Stripe's own policies and legal requirements.
