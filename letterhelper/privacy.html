Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

This policy explains what information Letter Helper ("we", "us") collects when you use our WhatsApp service, how we use it, and the choices you have. Letter Helper is operated by X Commerce LLC.

What we collect

  • Your messages and letters. The photos, text, and other messages you send to Letter Helper on WhatsApp, so we can translate them and reply.
  • A hashed phone identifier. We do not store your phone number in readable form. We store a one-way hashed version of it so we can recognize your account and track your usage.
  • Minimal usage data. Basic records such as how many letters you have translated and whether you have paid, so the service works correctly.
  • Payment information. Payments are handled by Stripe. We do not see or store your card number. Stripe may share with us limited details such as whether a payment succeeded.

How we use it

  • To translate your letters and send the translation back to you.
  • To create audio that reads your letter aloud, when you ask for it.
  • To keep track of your usage and payments.
  • To respond to you if you contact us.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We only share information with the services needed to run Letter Helper:

  • Meta / WhatsApp, which delivers messages between you and Letter Helper.
  • Google (Gemini AI), which processes the content of your letters to produce translations and audio.
  • Stripe, which processes payments.
  • Railway, our hosting provider, which runs our server and database.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy. We may also share information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep only what we need to provide the service. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see below).

Your choices and data deletion

To delete your data, email yim77708@gmail.com. We will delete your data within 30 days. See our Data Deletion Instructions for details.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including hashing phone identifiers. No online service can be guaranteed 100% secure.

Children

Letter Helper is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page.
